Where Do We Eat

Decides where to eat for the people you eat with.

Pick who's going, ask what sounds good, and get three choices that fit. Go Now logs the visit. A sentence like "Lunch with Shane at Taco Choza last Wednesday" logs one you already had. Plans go into your calendar through Apple's editor. Everything stays in your iCloud.
